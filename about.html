About Arputham Traders

One Man's Scrap, Another Man's Resource.

A Chennai-based, family-run scrap collection business turning unwanted metal, copper, aluminium, e‑waste and industrial scrap into fair value — for households and businesses alike.

Our Story

We Collect. We Recycle. We Build a Better Tomorrow.

Arputham Traders began as a simple, local scrap collection service in Ashok Nagar, Chennai, with one goal: make it easy and fair for people to turn what they no longer need into instant cash — without the hassle of finding a buyer, negotiating on the spot, or wondering if the weighing scale is honest.

Since then, we've grown to serve homes, offices, factories, warehouses and commercial spaces across the city, offering doorstep pickup and same-day payment for every kind of scrap — from a bundle of copper wire to a full industrial clean-out.

Our approach hasn't changed: show up on time, weigh accurately, pay fairly, and treat every customer the way we'd want to be treated.
