ARPUTHAM TRADERS - OFFLINE STATIC WEBSITE

To open the site on your computer:
1. Keep this whole folder together.
2. Double-click index.html.
3. Do not move index.html away from styles.css, script.js, favicon.svg, or the media folder.

The site works without a server. Home, About us, and Privacy & terms navigation uses the browser URL hash when opened directly from a file.

To add future photos or videos:
1. Put them inside the media folder.
2. Add the filename to the galleryItems list in script.js.
