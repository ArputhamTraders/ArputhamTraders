Privacy Policy

Arputham Traders ("we", "us", "our") operates as a scrap collection and recycling business based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through this website (arputhamtraders.work.gd), how we use it, and the choices you have. It is framed to be consistent with India's Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

Information We Collect

When you use our "Connect Now" form, call, WhatsApp or email us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address;
  • Your pickup address or locality;
  • Details about the scrap you wish to sell (type and approximate quantity);
  • Any other information you choose to share with us in your message.

We do not knowingly collect financial account details, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal data through this website.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to pickup requests and schedule collections;
  • Provide price quotes and confirm collection details over call or WhatsApp;
  • Maintain basic business records of enquiries and pickups;
  • Improve our service and communicate updates relevant to your request.

Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. Information you submit through our Connect Now form is received via Google Forms and may be visible to authorised personnel of Arputham Traders for the sole purpose of processing your request. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, or a valid request from a government or law-enforcement authority.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us from unauthorised access, alteration or misuse. Enquiry and pickup records are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for business, accounting or legal purposes, after which they may be deleted or anonymised.

Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to us at the contact details in the Grievance Officer section below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Cookies

This website may use basic cookies or similar local storage to remember your preferences and to understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so will not affect your ability to contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email.

Terms and Conditions

These Terms and Conditions govern your use of this website and any scrap collection or purchase services provided by Arputham Traders. By using this website or engaging our services, you agree to these terms.

Use of Services

Arputham Traders collects, weighs and purchases scrap materials including but not limited to metal scrap, copper, aluminium, e-waste and industrial scrap from households and businesses within our serviceable area. Pickup requests submitted through this website, by phone, or by WhatsApp are treated as an enquiry and are subject to confirmation by our team based on location, material type and schedule availability.

Pricing & Payment

Prices for scrap materials are based on prevailing market rates, material type, condition and quantity, and are subject to change without prior notice. Any price mentioned over call, WhatsApp or on this website prior to physical inspection and weighing is indicative only and not binding. The final price is determined at the time of pickup based on accurate on-the-spot weighing, and payment is made in cash (or another mutually agreed mode) at that time.

Customer Obligations

By requesting a pickup, you confirm that:

  • You are the lawful owner of the scrap material or are authorised to sell it;
  • The material does not include prohibited, hazardous, stolen or illegally obtained items;
  • You will provide accurate contact and address details so that pickup can be completed safely and on schedule.

Arputham Traders reserves the right to decline collection of any material that is unsafe, hazardous, or that we reasonably believe to be unlawfully obtained.
